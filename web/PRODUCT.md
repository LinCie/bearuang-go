# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

SME owners and self-hosters. The primary user is a business owner who wants to run a minimalist, reliable business management system, potentially on infrastructure they control themselves.

## Product Purpose

A minimalist and reliable ERP for small and medium-sized businesses. Success means giving business owners a dependable operational foundation that can grow into a full-featured ERP and POS while keeping money, stock, and daily work understandable.

## Positioning

Bearuang is a self-hostable ERP foundation for SMEs: steady, approachable, and practical about the work of protecting business money and operations. It prioritizes minimalism and reliability instead of beginning with the complexity of a broad enterprise suite.

## Brand

**Bearuang** combines *beruang* (bear) and *uang* (money). The bear is a behavioral metaphor for steadiness, protection, resilience, and warmth; the money half keeps that promise grounded in the resources an SME owner needs to see and manage clearly.

The brand should feel like a capable companion at the shop counter—not a financial service, a cartoon mascot, or a promise of wealth. Its personality is calm, grounded, friendly, and careful. Product language should be plain Indonesian, reassuring without being childish, and direct about the user's next action.

The Bearuang identity is successful when the product feels protective of the owner's time, attention, money, and stock even when no bear imagery is present.

## Operating Context

The product is intended to be self-hosted and used by SME owners to manage business operations. Detailed workflows, deployment environments, and operational rituals remain open decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current repository includes authentication and protected product-category, product, and product-variant management APIs.
- Bearuang is a product identity and metaphor, not a claim that the product provides financial services or financial advice.
- The intended roadmap is to expand toward a full-featured ERP and POS.
- Product language should be Indonesian.
- Monetary values should use Indonesian rupiah (IDR/Rp).
- Detailed module scope, roles, permissions, and integrations are undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

- Go API with PostgreSQL persistence in the repository.
- React Router web application scaffold in `web/`.
- No customer evidence, testimonials, brand assets, or other proof supplied yet. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Start minimal and useful.
- Favor reliability over novelty.
- Keep self-hosting practical.
- Grow the system incrementally toward ERP and POS breadth.
- Make money, stock, and operational state legible.
- Use the bear metaphor to signal care and steadiness, never to infantilize the user.
- Respect SME owners' time and operational context.
