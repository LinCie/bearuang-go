# Home route — first operational surface

## Scope

- **Primary target:** `web/app/routes/home.tsx`
- **Mode:** Operate
- **Role:** The first usable home surface for the SME operations workspace.

## Audience, job, action, proof, and constraints

- **Audience:** SME owners and self-hosters opening the system to orient themselves and continue daily work.
- **Job:** Understand what needs attention and reach catalog work without learning a complex ERP shell.
- **Primary action:** Start or continue managing the product catalog.
- **Proof/content:** Use live categories, products, variants, and operational states when available; never fabricate counts or business claims.
- **Constraints:** Indonesian UI copy, Rp formatting, clear accessibility states, responsive behavior, and a dependable low-cognitive-load experience.

## Memorable moment

The first glance should feel like finding the right line in a familiar ledger: the current state is clear, the next useful action is close, and nothing competes with it.

## Direction contract

### THESIS

Make the home route a friendly market ledger, not a KPI wall. It should orient an owner and point directly to useful catalog work instead of scattering attention across a dashboard mosaic.

### OWN-WORLD

Translate the assigned star-atlas structure into an autumnal operational almanac: a warm paper field, bark-dark ink, restrained toasted-leaf actions, and muted moss and dried-straw states. Use one legible humanist interface voice, continuous work surfaces, clear indexed groupings, and no literal astronomy or seasonal props.

### STORY

The visitor understands where the business workspace is, sees the live catalog state that matters, and trusts the next action because labels, values, and status are explicit. They should be able to enter product management without decoding a visual metaphor.

### FIRST VIEWPORT

On desktop, a compact orientation rail anchors the left edge while the main area opens with one Indonesian page title, one primary catalog action, and a short strip of live operational signals. A single focused catalog work surface follows. On mobile, the rail becomes a simple top bar; the title and action remain visible, and rows stack without losing their labels or actions.

### FORM

The Harvest Almanac: candidate 6 of the grounded direction set, seed key `6aac5f54`. Categories are landmarks, product groups are fields, and values are indexed for quick comparison. The structure must stay simple enough that the almanac is felt as order, never presented as an atlas gimmick.
